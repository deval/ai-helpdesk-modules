What is an LLM?

A large language model is not a database — it doesn't "look up" answers.

It's a statistical next-token predictor: trained on huge amounts of text, predicting the most likely next token given everything before it.

There is no internal fact-checker and no concept of "I don't know this."

An LLM optimizes for plausible, not true.

Watch hallucination happen live

Naive prompt:

Reply to this customer complaint politely.
Fails: "I've issued a full refund of $[amount]..."
— invents a refund amount and a policy decision no one made.

Fix it: grounding

Improved prompt — adds rules + reference facts:

Rules: - Only state facts present in the ticket or policy notes - Do NOT promise a refund amount - Acknowledge the specific complaint
Result: acknowledges the issue, states it qualifies for review — not a promised amount.
No invented facts.
